About us.

Summer Made Fresh is a local, family-owned food trailer built around one simple idea: great food and refreshing lemonade made with care. We’re fully insured and proud to serve our community with mouthwatering bites, fresh drinks, and a friendly atmosphere wherever we go. Whether it’s a small gathering or a big event, we bring the same energy, good vibes, and attention to quality every time. Our goal is to make every stop feel like summer — fresh, fun, and welcoming for everyone. From quick refreshments to delicious eats, Summer Made Fresh is here to make your day better.
